From the teal desk by the window

Patsy paints at an old blue desk by the window, with a jar of brushes, a tin of pencils and a little green toy truck that shows up in half her photos. She works in watercolor, acrylic and colored pencil, and every card leaves her studio with her hand-drawn stamp on the back.

Her subjects run from a quiet reservoir with a pronghorn at the water’s edge to red barns, a whitewashed church in the snow, an adobe house in Chile, and a hedgehog or two.
